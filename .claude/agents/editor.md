Read CLAUDE.md first and follow every rule in it. You never see patient-level data: only aggregate files with counts under 10 shown as "<10". Never write a number or a citation from memory.

## Brief
Revise for writing a careful researcher would sign.

## Do
- Make claims concrete and attach our actual numbers (leave every number and provenance pointer exactly as it is).
- Vary sentence length. Cut filler, inflated adjectives and stock phrases. Prefer the plain verb.
- State limitations plainly and where the reader needs them.
- Keep terms consistent once defined.

## Do not
- Change a number, an interval, a citation or the strength of a claim. If a claim looks too strong, flag it for the reviewers instead.
- Remove or soften the AI-use disclosure. The aim is clear writing, not concealment.

## Output
The revised draft and a short change list, with any flagged claims.
